What testing found

Bug found · fixed

The similarity guardrail alone wasn't enough

A soldering fault described in text scored 0.73 similarity to our cases — above the 0.70 threshold — so the retrieval check stayed silent and the result looked confident.

Fix: the model's own confidence is now a second, independent signal. Either one raises the warning.

“This may not be a dispensing defect we have on record.”

Result: 2 / 2 out-of-scope cases flagged

Known limitation

One honest miss

“Every so often a pad gets nothing at all” was diagnosed as Inconsistent Dispensing Volume (confidence 4) instead of Missing Dispensing Dots.

The correct case was retrieved as the top match. The model over-weighted “intermittent” against “nothing at all.”

Logged in the results file as a limitation — not hidden.
